# Source

`centerline.csv` and `raceline.csv` come from the TUM FTM racetrack database:
https://github.com/TUMFTM/racetrack-database (files `tracks/Melbourne.csv`, `racelines/Melbourne.csv`).
License: LGPL-3.0, see `LICENSE.txt`. Unmodified copies.

Columns: `x_m, y_m` centreline in metres (local planar frame, x east, y north),
`w_tr_right_m, w_tr_left_m` = distance from centreline to the right/left track edge.
No elevation data is included.
